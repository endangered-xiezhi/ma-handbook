## 第十课：五道高难度英文专业技术面试情境题

### 使用方法

先遮住示范答案，给自己 2–3 分钟口头回答。每题至少说清：结论、规则、关键事实缺口、立即采取的交易动作、无法解决时的备选方案。法规结论须按交易发生时点核验；以下是学习示范，不替代具体交易法律意见。

### Case 1：未缴出资、加速到期与估值

**Interviewer's Prompt**

> We are acquiring 70% of a PRC target. Its registered capital is RMB 100 million, but only RMB 20 million has been paid. The remaining contributions are scheduled over the next eight years. The seller says the buyer will simply assume the unpaid amount. How would you advise us on valuation, closing conditions and liability allocation?

**Core Issues & Traps**：核实章程、股东名册、实缴凭证、出资期限和是否存在公司不能清偿到期债务等加速到期事实；区分交易前后责任及《公司法》第88条适用；检查《公司法》第47条五年出资期限和国务院令第784号存量公司过渡规则。不要把“买方接手”当成唯一答案，也不要忽略目标公司本身可能负有催缴义务（第51、52条）。

**Candidate's Golden Answer**

> **BLUF:** I would not accept the seller’s assumption without quantifying the unpaid contribution and testing whether any amount is already due or accelerated. The unpaid capital is both a valuation issue and a potential post-Closing liability. I would confirm the Target’s incorporation date, articles, contribution schedule, payment records and any creditor or company demands, and then apply the transitional rules in force for that company.
>
> Under the PRC Company Law, the contribution timetable and consequences depend on the company’s status and the relevant statutory provisions. Article 54 may permit acceleration in specified circumstances where the company is unable to pay debts as they fall due. Articles 51 and 52 address verification, demand and forfeiture procedures. Article 88 allocates liability in a transfer of equity where the contribution period has not expired or the contribution is overdue; its application must be assessed against the transaction date and relevant judicial interpretation.
>
> Commercially, I would model the unpaid amount as a debt-like or equity-like adjustment, depending on the valuation methodology. Before Closing, the preferred solution may be payment by the responsible shareholder or a properly implemented capital reduction, subject to corporate approvals and creditor protection. Residual exposure should be covered by a specific indemnity and escrow. The SPA should state that contractual allocation does not release a person from statutory liability to the company or its creditors.

### Case 2：隐名持股与境外买方收购

**Interviewer's Prompt**

> During diligence, we discover that the registered shareholder holds 30% of the target for an undisclosed beneficial owner. The beneficial owner funded the investment and now refuses to sign the transfer documents. The target also has a historical equity pledge entry. Can we close and rely on an indemnity?

**Core Issues & Traps**：区分代持协议在内部关系与对公司、债权人及其他第三方的效果；核实登记股东、实际出资人、质押和执行状态；检查外资准入、税务和登记。不要断言代持当然无效，也不要认为赔偿能替代有效转让或消灭第三方权利。

**Candidate's Golden Answer**

> **BLUF:** I would not close on an indemnity alone. Closing should be conditional on a verified ownership chain, release of the registered pledge and a legally effective transfer or restructuring, unless the buyer deliberately accepts a different perimeter.
>
> We would review the nominee agreement, payment trail, articles, shareholder resolutions, register of members, pledge registration and creditor notices. The nominee arrangement may have different effects between the nominee and beneficial owner and against the company, creditors or third parties. We would also confirm whether the beneficial owner is eligible to hold the interest under current foreign-investment rules.
>
> I would make the clean-up a Condition Precedent, require all relevant parties to execute transfer and release documents, and complete corporate, tax and registration steps. The SPA should include title warranties, a specific indemnity for historic claims and a no-further-encumbrance covenant. Escrow improves recovery only; it does not cure defective title. If cooperation cannot be obtained, delay, restructure or termination should remain available.

### Case 3：境外贷款人、A 股质押与违约处置

**Interviewer's Prompt**

> A Hong Kong lender has made a cross-border loan to a PRC borrower secured by shares in a PRC-listed company. The borrower defaults. The loan agreement says the lender may automatically take ownership of the shares and remit the value offshore. Is that enforceable, and what is the practical enforcement route?

**Core Issues & Traps**：分别分析担保登记、CSDC 质押、流质限制、证券处置、境外主体受让资格、SAFE/银行购汇和汇出。不要把“只能司法拍卖”或“登记即保证可以汇出”说成绝对结论。

**Candidate's Golden Answer**

> **BLUF:** I would not assume that automatic appropriation and offshore remittance are enforceable as drafted. We must separate pledge perfection, lawful enforcement, the lender’s eligibility to acquire or sell the shares, and conversion and remittance of proceeds.
>
> Under the PRC Civil Code, the pledge is generally perfected through the relevant registration, while a pre-default arrangement automatically transferring collateral to the creditor is subject to the forfeiture prohibition. After default, the parties may need a lawful sale, auction or other permitted route, depending on the share type, listing rules, lender status and current CSDC procedures.
>
> We would also classify the cross-border guarantee under the applicable SAFE rules and confirm registration or reporting. Enforcement proceeds do not automatically become freely convertible or remittable. The documents should preserve lawful enforcement options and make conversion and remittance subject to the applicable bank, SAFE, tax and securities requirements.

### Case 4：50:50 JV 僵局与关联交易

**Interviewer's Prompt**

> Our US client owns 50% of a PRC joint venture. The board is deadlocked, and the Chinese partner caused the JV to enter into an overpriced supply contract with its affiliate without meaningful disclosure. The client wants to dissolve the JV and open a WFOE next month. What would you recommend?

**Core Issues & Traps**：先看章程、合资合同和僵局机制；核查关联交易披露、回避、审批和定价；司法解散是法定门槛下的最后手段；WFOE 不会自动承接 JV 的牌照、员工、合同、资产或数据。

**Candidate's Golden Answer**

> **BLUF:** I would preserve the JV’s value and address the conflicted transaction before treating dissolution as the first step. We should stop further value leakage, secure records, test the contract and approvals, and activate the contractual deadlock ladder.
>
> We would review voting thresholds, reserved matters, escalation and buy-sell rights. For the affiliate contract, we would test disclosure, recusal, pricing, approvals and performance against the directors’ and controlling persons’ duties. Depending on the facts, the remedies may include renegotiation or unwinding, independent review, a shareholder or derivative claim, negotiated buy-out, or judicial dissolution if the statutory conditions are met.
>
> A WFOE may be a business alternative, but it does not erase JV liabilities. We would separately map customer and supplier consents, employees, licences, land, assets, data and regulatory filings before moving any business.

### Case 5：国家安全审查与关联并购

**Interviewer's Prompt**

> A foreign investor plans to acquire a Chinese technology company through a Hong Kong vehicle. The target supplies critical-infrastructure components. The parties propose transferring the shares to a friendly PRC nominee and then selling them offshore to avoid national security review and the related-party restrictions in the M&A Rules. Can we proceed?

**Core Issues & Traps**：核实国家安全审查行业和控制权/实际影响；穿透最终受益人、治理权和资金路径；分别检查负面清单、行业许可、反垄断和 10 号文第11、16条。不能把“过桥换籍、买断重组”写成当然规避方案。

**Candidate's Golden Answer**

> **BLUF:** I would not recommend a nominee or temporary transfer designed to disguise the transaction. Regulators may assess substance, control and actual influence, and the structure creates enforceability, disclosure and regulatory risks.
>
> We should map the full ownership chain, ultimate beneficial owners, board rights, vetoes, financing and contractual control. The target’s technology and customers should be tested against the sectors and triggers under the current Foreign Investment Security Review Measures. Separately, we would assess the Negative List, sector licensing, merger control and the related-party rules under the M&A Rules, including Article 11 where applicable. Article 16 and any contribution timetable must be checked against the law in force for the transaction.
>
> The compliant path is a transparent review strategy, a realistic timetable, and an SPA making any required review or clearance a Condition Precedent with cooperation covenants and a long-stop/termination mechanism. A genuinely different structure can be considered only after confirming that it complies with the applicable rules; a paper intermediary does not remove substantive scrutiny.

### 面试后复盘表

| 检查项 | 自评问题 |
|---|---|
| 结论先行 | 我是否在前两句回答了能否继续、最先做什么？ |
| 规则准确 | 我是否说明条文适用条件和交易时点，而非只报条号？ |
| 事实应用 | 我是否指出还需要查哪些文件和事实？ |
| 商业落地 | 我是否提出 CP、covenant、赔偿、托管、替代结构或退出方案？ |
| 表达边界 | 我是否避免“一律、绝对、自动”等无条件断言？ |

**记忆总式：**

> **Answer → Rule → Apply → Allocate → Verify → Fallback.**

## 第十三课：中英文法规表达卡

以下句子用于英文笔试和专业面试，重点是准确表达法律分析，不是逐字翻译法条标题。

| 中文表达 | 推荐英文表达 | 适用位置 |
|---|---|---|
| 在适用法律允许的范围内 | to the extent permitted by applicable law | 合同保留句 |
| 以取得审批为交割先决条件 | make the approval a Condition Precedent to Closing | SPA/备忘录 |
| 需向主管机关核验 | should be confirmed with the competent authority | 法律意见边界 |
| 需向经办银行核验材料 | should be confirmed with the handling bank | SAFE/购汇 |
| 不应作绝对结论 | should not be stated as an absolute conclusion | 风险提示 |
| 视交易时点而定 | depends on the transaction date | 历史题/过渡法 |
| 视主体和资金路径而定 | depends on the entities involved and the funds flow | 外债/担保 |
| 受负面清单限制 | subject to the restrictions in the Negative List | FDI |
| 适用准入前国民待遇 | subject to pre-establishment national treatment | FDI |
| 穿透最终受益人 | trace through to the ultimate beneficial owner | NSR/反洗钱 |
| 实际控制或实际影响 | control or actual influence in substance | NSR |
| 未缴出资责任 | liability for unpaid capital contribution | 公司法 |
| 加速到期 | acceleration of the contribution obligation | 公司法第54条 |
| 股权转让责任 | liability arising from a transfer of equity | 公司法第88条 |
| 解除质押 | release of the share pledge | Closing deliverable |
| 质押登记 | pledge registration / registration for perfection | CSDC |
| 合法处置担保物 | lawfully enforce and dispose of the collateral | 融资/违约 |
| 购汇和跨境汇出 | purchase of foreign exchange and remittance abroad | SAFE |
| 资金用途限制 | restrictions on the use of proceeds | 外债/DCM |
| 充分且具体披露 | fairly and specifically disclosed | Disclosure Letter |
| 交割日重复确认 | bring-down at Closing | R&W |
| 不成比例影响回拨 | disproportionate-effect carve-back | MAE |
| 不得重复受偿 | no double recovery | Indemnity |
| 赔偿可回收性 | recoverability of the indemnity | Escrow |
| 监管延迟 | regulatory delay | Long-stop |
| 替代交易结构 | alternative transaction structure | 备选方案 |

### 口答万能四句

> **The starting point is the transaction structure and the relevant date.**
>
> **We should separate mandatory legal or regulatory conditions from risks that can be allocated contractually.**
>
> **I would verify the position with the relevant authority, registry or handling bank and reflect the result in the SPA or offering documents.**
>
> **If the gating issue cannot be resolved by the long-stop date, the client should retain a commercially workable fallback or termination right.**

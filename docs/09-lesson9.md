## 第九课：英文笔试怎样在两小时内写出合伙人愿意读的答案

### 1. 先理解笔试真正考什么

外资律所的非诉笔试通常不是单纯考“记住多少条文”。阅卷人更关心四件事：

1. 你能否从长事实中找出真正影响交易的风险；
2. 你能否区分现行法、历史规则、监管实操和商业判断；
3. 你能否把法律结论转化为 CP、covenant、价格调整、赔偿或终止机制；
4. 你的英文是否清楚、克制、可供客户或合伙人直接使用。

因此，答案不应写成法规摘抄，也不应写成没有结论的研究笔记。一个合格的第一句通常直接回答客户最想知道的问题：**Can we proceed, what must be fixed first, and how should the residual risk be allocated?**

### 2. 两小时笔试时间表

| 时间 | 动作 | 产出 |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 分钟 | 通读题目，圈出主体、交易动作、日期、金额、监管机关和问题动词 | 一页事实地图 |
| 10–20 分钟 | 列出 Issue 清单，按“能否做、何时做、谁承担、失败怎么办”排序 | 3–6 个核心问题 |
| 20–35 分钟 | 查找题目提供的法规或凭记忆搭规则骨架，标记不确定时点 | Rule 表 |
| 35–75 分钟 | 按 IRAC/CREAC 写主体分析，先写高风险问题 | 初稿主体 |
| 75–95 分钟 | 加入交易动作、文件条款、替代结构和监管核验事项 | Advice/Recommendation |
| 95–110 分钟 | 做英文编辑和 redline 检查 | 可交付版本 |
| 110–120 分钟 | 逐项核对问题是否回答、法条是否过度断言、是否遗漏日期和责任人 | 最终稿 |

**时间管理原则**：如果某一监管细节无法确认，先写清楚“需要向主管机关或经办银行核验”，继续完成交易逻辑。不要在一个不确定点上耗掉整场考试。

### 3. 先画一张事实地图

读题时不要立即写英文长句，先把事实放进五个框：

| 框 | 要问的问题 | 常见英文标签 |
|---|---|---|
| Parties | 谁买、谁卖、谁控制、谁担保？ | Buyer, Seller, Target, Lender |
| Transaction | 买的是股权、资产、股份发行还是债券？ | share deal, asset deal, issuance |
| Timing | 事实发生在签约前、签约后还是交割后？ | pre-signing, interim, closing |
| Regulation | 哪个机关、哪项登记、哪种许可可能卡住交易？ | SAMR, SAFE, CSDC, NSR |
| Remedy | 风险应由谁解决、赔偿、托管还是允许退出？ | CP, covenant, indemnity, escrow |

然后把每个事实改写成一个问题。例如：

- “股东还没有缴足认缴出资” → **Who bears the contribution liability after transfer, and must it be cured before Closing?**
- “境外银行接受 A 股股票质押” → **How is the pledge perfected, how may it be enforced, and can sale proceeds be converted and remitted?**
- “50:50 JV 董事会无法决策” → **What deadlock mechanism exists, and when would a buy-out or judicial dissolution become realistic?**

### 4. IRAC、CREAC 和 BLUF 怎么组合

这三个词不是三种互相排斥的格式。

- **BLUF（Bottom Line Up Front）**：第一段先说结论和建议；
- **IRAC（Issue–Rule–Application–Conclusion）**：适合逐个问题展开；
- **CREAC（Conclusion–Rule–Explanation–Application–Conclusion）**：适合较长法律备忘录，每一部分先后都有小结论。

初学者可以使用下面的组合：

```text
BLUF：先给交易结论和优先级
Issue：明确问题及时间点
Rule：列适用法、合同条款和监管要求
Application：把题目事实逐项套入规则
Recommendation：给出 CP、covenant、indemnity 或替代结构
Conclusion：说明能否继续及下一步
```

### 5. 一个可直接套用的英文骨架

> **BLUF.** The transaction can proceed in principle, but we would not recommend Closing until [critical issue] is resolved or expressly allocated in the transaction documents. The immediate priorities are [priority 1], [priority 2] and [priority 3].
>
> **Issue and rule.** The first issue is whether [fact] prevents the Target from lawfully operating or the parties from completing the transaction. Under [law/regulation/contract provision], [short rule]. The position may depend on the transaction date, the entity’s status and the practice of the relevant authority, which should be confirmed before signing.
>
> **Application.** On the stated facts, [apply each material fact]. This creates [legal/commercial consequence]. A general warranty would not by itself solve [gating issue], because it does not provide the required approval, registration or licence.
>
> **Recommendation.** We would make [approval/cure/release] a Condition Precedent, require [interim conduct] as a covenant, and cover the residual exposure with a Specific Indemnity and [escrow/price adjustment/parent guarantee]. The SPA should also include a long-stop date and a termination mechanism if the condition is not satisfied.
>
> **Conclusion.** Subject to those steps and confirmation from [relevant authority/bank/registry], the structure is workable. We would not describe the outcome as automatic because [key uncertainty].

### 6. 真题型示范一：隐名股权代持

**事实简化**：境外买方拟收购一家中国公司。尽调发现登记股东只是名义持有人，实际出资人另有其人；目标公司股权还存在历史质押记录。

**低分写法**：

> The nominee shareholding is illegal. The Buyer should terminate the deal.

问题在于结论过度绝对，没有区分内部协议效力、登记外观、第三方执行、质押状态和交易补救。

**合伙人级写法**：

> **BLUF:** We should not treat the nominee arrangement as a single yes-or-no issue. Closing should be conditional on establishing a legally effective ownership chain, releasing the historical pledge and obtaining all required corporate, registration, tax and foreign-investment clearances. If that cannot be achieved by the long-stop date, the Buyer should retain a termination right or reconsider the transaction perimeter.
>
> **Analysis:** The arrangement may have different effects between the nominee and beneficial owner and against the company, creditors or other third parties. We would verify the trust or nominee agreement, payment evidence, shareholder resolutions, articles, equity register, pledge registration and any creditor claims. We would also identify whether the beneficial owner is eligible to hold the relevant interest under the applicable foreign-investment rules. A private acknowledgement alone would not necessarily cure the registration or third-party risk.
>
> **Documentation:** The Seller should procure a pre-Closing clean-up involving the nominee, beneficial owner and Target, with transfer or confirmation documents, release of the pledge, tax analysis and required filings. The SPA should contain a title warranty, a specific indemnity for historic claims and a covenant prohibiting further disposal or encumbrance. If the clean-up is incomplete, an escrow may improve recoverability but should not be presented as a substitute for a legally effective transfer.

### 7. 真题型示范二：JV 僵局与关联交易

面对 50:50 JV，先不要跳到“立即解散”。按以下顺序问：

1. 董事会和股东会的表决门槛是什么？
2. 合资合同、章程或股东协议有没有升级、调解、买卖选择权或独立董事机制？
3. 中方关联交易是否经过回避表决、定价是否符合独立交易原则、资金是否已经流出？
4. 是否存在损害公司利益的行为，可由公司或符合条件的股东采取公司法上的救济？
5. 另设 WFOE 是否真的可行，是否会触发牌照、客户、员工、土地、数据或反垄断问题？

英文回答可写成：

> **The commercial objective should be to restore governability before pursuing dissolution.** We would first apply the contractual deadlock ladder and preserve evidence concerning the related-party transaction. The transaction should be tested against the directors’ and controlling persons’ duties, the approval and recusal process, arm’s-length pricing and any diversion of corporate opportunity. Depending on the facts, the remedies may include reversal or ratification, an independent review, shareholder or derivative proceedings, a negotiated buy-out, or ultimately judicial dissolution where the statutory threshold is met. Establishing a separate WFOE may be a business alternative, but it does not erase the JV’s liabilities, licences, employees or regulatory obligations.

### 8. Redline：初学者逐条检查方法

Redline 不是“把英文改得更漂亮”，而是判断每一处修改会不会改变风险分配。审查时按四遍进行：

**第一遍：实体和定义。** 检查 Buyer、Seller、Company、Shares、Loss、Encumbrance、Business Day 是否统一；是否把 “Closing” 误改成 “Signing”。

**第二遍：责任强度。** 比较 shall、may、best endeavours、reasonable endeavours、to the extent、material、actual knowledge。一个词可能改变责任范围和诉讼风险。

**第三遍：时间和条件。** 检查签约日、交割日、Long-stop Date、索赔期限、付款日、通知期和监管等待期是否前后一致。

**第四遍：救济和冲突。** 确认 R&W、披露、专项赔偿、托管、价格调整、终止和争议解决之间没有互相削弱或重复赔偿。

常见 redline 例子：

| 原文 | 修改 | 修改效果 |
|---|---|---|
| “The Seller shall use efforts” | “The Seller shall use reasonable endeavours” | 明确最低努力标准，但仍需结合适用法解释 |
| “any Losses” | “all Losses reasonably incurred and finally determined” | 缩小到合理且确定的损失，卖方更有利 |
| “disclosed in the Data Room” | “fairly and specifically disclosed in Schedule 3” | 限制一揽子数据室披露，买方更有利 |
| “may terminate” | “may terminate by written notice” | 增加终止程序要求 |
| “to the Seller’s knowledge” | 定义 Knowledge Persons 和 reasonable enquiries | 防止知识限定没有边界 |

### 9. 四类高频错误

1. **只报法条，不回答商业问题**：写完“根据第 X 条”后没有说谁做什么、何时做、失败怎么办。
2. **把历史题规则当现行法**：旧题可能出现审批、备案、外汇登记和外资限制；必须标注题目年份，并核对现行规则。
3. **把风险赔偿当作许可替代品**：钱不能替代强制审批、登记、牌照或股权有效转让。
4. **结论过度绝对**：跨境交易常受实体、行业、资金路径、主管机关和时点影响，应使用 “subject to”, “depending on”, “we would confirm”。

### 10. 第九课自测卡

**题 1：** 题目给出一项未足额出资事实，你的答案第一句应写什么？

**答题方向：** 先判断它是否影响合法交割或目标持续经营，再说明补缴、减资、价格调整、专项赔偿和托管的组合，而不是立即断言交易无效。

**题 2：** 为什么 “The Seller should comply with all laws” 不是好的 R&W？

**答题方向：** 范围过宽、难以核实、没有时间和重大性边界，也没有指出许可、税务、环保、数据等具体风险。应拆成可验证的模块。

**题 3：** redline 中把 “fairly and specifically disclosed” 改成 “disclosed” 会产生什么问题？

**答题方向：** 可能让任何形式的披露都限定保证，买方难以主张卖方没有清楚说明风险；应结合数据室、披露函和具体披露标准判断。

**题 4：** 面试官问“Can we close tomorrow?”，如何回答？

**参考句**：

> **Not on the current facts. I would first confirm whether the required approvals, releases, registrations and closing deliverables are complete. If any of them is legally mandatory, it cannot simply be waived by contract. If the remaining issue is only a compensable commercial risk, we could consider an indemnity or escrow, but that would not replace a mandatory approval or registration.**

**记忆口诀：**

> **先结论，后规则；先卡点，后条款；事实要落证据，风险要落责任人；不确定就标时点、机关和核验动作。**

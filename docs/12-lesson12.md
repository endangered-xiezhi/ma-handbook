## 第十二课：14 天每日一题训练营

### 使用规则

每天用 20–30 分钟完成一题：

1. **3 分钟闭卷列点**：只写事实、风险、规则、动作四栏；
2. **5 分钟口头回答**：先说中文结论，再用英文说三句话；
3. **10 分钟核对**：对照参考骨架，补上遗漏的责任人、文件和时间点；
4. **2 分钟打卡**：给自己标记 A（完整）、B（漏一项）、C（需要重做）。

参考答案是“骨架”，不是唯一答案。实际交易仍需核对法律有效期、行业规则和主管机关口径。

### Day 1：股权收购还是资产收购？

**题目**：境外买方想收购一家拥有生产许可证和土地使用权的制造企业。目标公司还有历史环保欠款。你会先问什么？

**记忆骨架**：先比较历史负债、牌照接续、土地房产、员工、税务和合同；股权收购保留经营连续性但承继历史风险；资产收购可隔离部分负债但需要逐项转移和重新确认。建议做五维矩阵，再决定交易边界。

**英文三句**：

> I would begin with the liability perimeter, licences and asset-transfer feasibility. A share deal preserves continuity but carries the target’s historical liabilities; an asset deal may ring-fence liabilities but requires separate transfers and approvals. The recommendation should follow a five-factor comparison of liabilities, permits, employees, tax and execution risk.

### Day 2：未缴出资如何影响估值？

**题目**：目标公司注册资本 1 亿元，实缴 2,000 万元，剩余出资八年后到期。卖方要求按完整注册资本估值。

**记忆骨架**：核对章程、成立日期、过渡规则、出资凭证和是否有加速到期事实；把未缴金额作为估值调整或交割前补缴/减资议题；剩余风险放入专项赔偿和托管；合同安排不能免除对公司和债权人的法定责任。

**英文三句**：

> The unpaid contribution is a valuation and liability issue, not merely an accounting entry. We should verify the due date, any acceleration trigger and the applicable transitional rules. I would seek cure or a lawful capital reduction before Closing and allocate residual exposure through a specific indemnity and escrow.

### Day 3：代持股权能否直接交割？

**题目**：登记股东承认其只是名义持有人，实际出资人拒绝签署转让文件。

**记忆骨架**：分内部代持关系和对公司、债权人、质权人的外部效力；核实付款、股东名册、章程、质押和冻结；把显名、转让、解除负担、税务和外资资格列为 CP；赔偿不能替代有效权属。

**英文三句**：

> I would not close on an indemnity alone. The buyer needs a verified title chain, release of encumbrances and legally effective transfer documents. If the beneficial owner will not cooperate, the buyer should delay, restructure or terminate rather than treat escrow as a substitute for title.

### Day 4：外资准入和负面清单

**题目**：外资拟投资一个受行业许可监管的中国业务，但项目不确定是否属于负面清单限制领域。

**记忆骨架**：先确定投资者、行业分类、持股比例、控制权、经营范围和许可；负面清单内看限制/禁止，清单外适用准入前国民待遇但仍有许可、信息报告、NSR 和反垄断；把必要审查或许可设为 CP。

**英文三句**：

> The Negative List is only the first gate. We must classify the business, ownership and control rights, then check sector licensing, information reporting, national security review and merger control. Any mandatory clearance should be an objective Condition Precedent with a realistic long-stop date.

### Day 5：国家安全审查是否可用 nominee 规避？

**题目**：交易团队提出先把股权转给境内友好股东，再由香港公司收购，以降低 NSR 风险。

**记忆骨架**：穿透最终受益人、治理权、否决权、资金和实际影响；实质重于形式，过桥换籍不是当然豁免；应采用透明申报、信息隔离、审查 CP 和终止机制。

**英文三句**：

> I would not recommend a nominee structure designed to disguise the investment. The review should consider substance, control and actual influence, not only the immediate registered holder. We should pursue a transparent review strategy and make any required clearance a Condition Precedent.

### Day 6：内保外贷、外保内贷怎么分？

**题目**：境内公司为境外借款人提供担保，贷款人是香港银行。客户问这是不是外保内贷。

**记忆骨架**：先看借款人所在地和资金实际流向，再分类；境内担保、境外借款通常进入内保外贷分析，境外担保、境内借款则进入外保内贷分析；随后分别检查 SAFE 登记/报送、履约购汇和银行材料。

**英文三句**：

> Classification depends on the location of the borrower, the guarantor and the underlying loan flow. A domestic guarantee for an offshore loan is analysed as an inward guarantee for an offshore loan, while an offshore guarantee for a domestic loan is a different category. We would then verify the applicable SAFE registration or reporting and the bank’s enforcement and purchase-of-foreign-exchange procedures.

### Day 7：全口径外债额度

**题目**：境内集团准备新增一笔境外股东贷款。你会先查哪些数字？

**记忆骨架**：查净资产、现有跨境融资余额、期限和币种、集团口径、宏观审慎参数及资金用途；不要只看单笔贷款金额；另行确认 NDRC 中长期外债审核登记是否适用。

**英文三句**：

> We would calculate headroom under the applicable macro-prudential framework using the relevant net-asset and outstanding-financing figures. The analysis must include the group perimeter, currency and maturity, and must be separated from any NDRC filing or approval for medium- and long-term foreign debt. Use of proceeds and registration evidence should be documented before drawdown.

### Day 8：A 股股票质押违约

**题目**：贷款协议写明违约后股票自动归境外银行所有，并可直接汇出价值。

**记忆骨架**：拆成四问：质押是否有效登记、自动归属是否触及流质限制、境外主体能否受让或出售、所得能否购汇汇出；设计合法折价/拍卖/变卖路径，并向 CSDC、经办银行和 SAFE 核验。

**英文三句**：

> The automatic-transfer wording should not be accepted without analysis. We need to separate pledge perfection, lawful enforcement, the lender’s eligibility to acquire or sell the shares, and the conversion and remittance of proceeds. The documents should preserve permitted sale or auction routes and make remittance subject to applicable securities and foreign-exchange requirements.

### Day 9：SPA 的 CP、covenant 和 R&W

**题目**：目标许可证将在签约后两个月到期，续期结果不确定。

**记忆骨架**：若无证不能合法经营，把续证或替代经营安排列为 CP；卖方在过渡期申请和维持许可证是 covenant；许可证历史和现状写入 R&W；已知损失可用专项赔偿和托管，但不能替代强制许可。

**英文三句**：

> If the licence is essential to lawful operation, renewal or an agreed alternative should be a Condition Precedent. The Seller should covenant to pursue renewal and maintain the business pending Closing, supported by a targeted warranty and indemnity for historic defects. Escrow may secure money claims but cannot replace a mandatory licence.

### Day 10：MAE 和 carve-outs

**题目**：行业整体下滑 15%，目标公司下滑 25%，买方想主张 MAE。

**记忆骨架**：看 MAE 定义、影响对象、持续性、行业 carve-out 和不成比例影响回拨；不能只看百分比；还要确认 MAE 是 CP、终止触发点还是 bring-down 标准。

**英文三句**：

> A percentage decline alone does not establish an MAE. We would read the definition, the industry carve-out and any disproportionate-effect carve-back, and test duration, causation and the impact on the target as a whole. We would also confirm whether the MAE operates as a Condition Precedent, termination trigger or warranty bring-down test.

### Day 11：已知风险和 sandbagging

**题目**：买方在数据室看到许可证问题，但卖方没有在披露函中具体列出，买方仍然交割。

**记忆骨架**：看 SPA 的披露标准、数据室是否构成披露、买方实际知识、是否 pro/anti-sandbagging；区分“看到文件”和“知道保证不真实及损失”；中国法下不机械作一刀切结论。

**英文三句**：

> The answer starts with the agreed disclosure and sandbagging wording. We would distinguish a document being uploaded from a sufficiently specific disclosure and distinguish awareness of a document from knowledge that a warranty is false and loss has arisen. The analysis must also consider the parties’ conduct, good faith and any deliberate concealment.

### Day 12：ECM 招股书行业概览

**题目**：客户只提供一段“行业高速增长、市场空间巨大”的宣传稿。

**记忆骨架**：补市场规模口径、时间、来源、竞争、周期、监管、增长驱动和发行人位置；每项关键数字要有可追溯来源；避免把营销语句当作披露事实。

**英文三句**：

> An industry section should be evidence-based rather than promotional. We would verify the market definition, period, source, competitive landscape, regulatory drivers and the issuer’s position. Any material statement should be traceable to reliable public or diligence materials and should be consistent with the issuer’s own operating data.

### Day 13：DCM 债券违约

**题目**：发行人漏付一期利息，投资人问是否立即加速到期。

**记忆骨架**：查债券条款中的宽限期、违约事件、通知、受托管理、持有人会议和加速门槛；再看交叉违约、担保和偿债顺序；不能只看募集说明书标题。

**英文三句**：

> We would read the bond terms, trustee agreement and applicable holder-meeting procedures before advising on acceleration. The key questions are whether the missed payment is an Event of Default, whether a grace period applies, who may declare acceleration and what voting threshold is required. We would also review cross-default, security enforcement and distribution priorities.

### Day 14：综合口答

**题目**：境外买方收购一家有未缴出资、代持、股票质押和外债的中国目标，且业务属于敏感技术行业。请在两分钟内给出行动顺序。

**记忆骨架**：顺序固定为 **结构—准入—权属—资金—合同—退出**：确定股权/资产结构；查 FDI/NSR/反垄断；清理代持和质押；拆分外债/担保/证券/汇款；用 CP、covenant、R&W、专项赔偿和 escrow 配置风险；设 long-stop 和 termination。

**英文三句**：

> I would run six workstreams in sequence: transaction perimeter, foreign-investment and security-review access, title and encumbrance clean-up, cross-border financing and foreign-exchange mechanics, SPA risk allocation, and fallback or termination. We should not rely on indemnities to cure mandatory approvals or defective title. The SPA should contain objective Conditions Precedent, interim covenants, targeted indemnities, escrow where useful, and a realistic long-stop date.
